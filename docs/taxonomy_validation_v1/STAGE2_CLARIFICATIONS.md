# Stage 2 annotation clarifications

These clarify the annotation procedure without changing any canonical category,
definition, ID, schema, or decision threshold. They contain no earlier case texts,
annotations or expected answers. Apply them with VALIDATION_PROMPT.md.

1. **Read the text before recognizing a form.** A calendar month is not an
   epistemic modal; a probability comparison is not automatically a hedge over
   an assertion; a word inside an idiom is not automatically a quantifier.
   Annotate function and scope in context, not string matches.
2. **Keep grammatical scale separate from discourse function.** Coordination
   inside a noun/adjective phrase need not connect propositions or discourse
   moves. A clause can be matrix, copular, active, passive or participial in
   different respects; do not classify a whole sentence from one embedded form.
3. **Use the right level/dimension from the full catalogue.** A sequence marker
   is not necessarily compression or enumeration. An explanatory reason need
   not be a contrast or scope caveat. If an apparent gap is really a wrong field,
   put the observation in its appropriate field rather than forcing an unrelated
   category. Preserve multiple independent supported features as separate entries.
4. **Bind quantities locally.** Link each number, date, comparison and negation
   to the proposition and entity it actually modifies. Do not attach all numbers
   in a shared sentence or paragraph to every claim. Preserve separately stated
   cohorts, counts and time windows.
5. **Build claim and relation IDs from this text.** Retain the governing matrix
   proposition and the scope of conditions or subordinate clauses. A syntactic
   fragment is not automatically an independent asserted claim. An association,
   hypothetical condition or possible consequence must not become verified causation.
6. **Check a relation's endpoints, direction and evidence together.** A valid ID
   with an unrelated anchor is not a valid binding. Do not manufacture an
   inferential connective or borrow a relation from another clause to justify a
   rhetorical label. New source-supported IDs are allowed in this fresh extraction;
   unsupported semantic links must remain unresolved.
7. **Uncertainty is an acceptable result.** Keep ambiguous passive/adjectival
   readings, missing context and inseparable qualification scopes explicit.
   Do not label every candidate or add a category merely to avoid abstention.

The task annotates the supplied text as written. It does not accept a neutral
rewrite, execute a control, change a claim or verify an author's intention.
